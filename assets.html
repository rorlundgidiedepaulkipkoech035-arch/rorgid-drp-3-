Protected assets

How it works

Upload a logo or marketing image. RORGID hides a unique serial number inside its pixels (invisible to the eye) and gives you a protected PNG to use on your website and adverts. If that image, or an exact copy, is found on a suspicious site, the platform recognises your serial and records it as evidence.

The hidden mark survives copying and lossless re-saving. It does not survive JPEG re-compression, resizing, cropping or screenshots, so exact-copy matching is used alongside it.
